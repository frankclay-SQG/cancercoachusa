Text messaging

SMS Terms

What to expect when you agree to receive text messages from Cancer Coach USA, and how to stop them at any time.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Program Name and Description

Cancer Coach USA Text Messages are sent by Cancer Coach USA (Dr. Danielle Clay), PO Box 2704, Chester, VA 23831. We text people who have asked to hear from us by text. Messages may include appointment confirmations and reminders for coaching sessions you book, replies to questions you send us, and updates about Cancer Coach USA meetups, gatherings, and survivorship resources.

How You Opt In

You agree to receive text messages when you do one of the following:

  • Check the box that reads “I agree to receive text messages from Cancer Coach USA” on the intake form or meetup registration form on this website. The box is never checked for you.
  • Book a session with Dr. Dani through her online booking page and provide your mobile number, which is used for texts about that appointment.
  • Text us first at (804) 312-8040.

Agreeing to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Message Frequency and Costs

Message frequency varies with your appointments and the updates you asked for. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to Opt Out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message from Cancer Coach USA. We will send one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further texts unless you opt in again. You can also reply CANCEL, END, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE, or ask us by email.

How to Get Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at support@cancercoachusa.com or (804) 312-8040.

Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed.

Privacy

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, except service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf. See our Privacy Policy SMS Terms for details.

Medical Information

Please do not send urgent medical information by text. Cancer Coach USA does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For emergencies, call 911.

Changes and Contact

We may update these SMS Terms. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Questions may be sent to support@cancercoachusa.com.
