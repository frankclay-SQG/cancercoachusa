Meetup group

A place for survivors and care takers to connect.

The Cancer Coach USA Meetup group is for cancer survivors and their care takers who want a supportive place to connect, share stories, and find encouragement after treatment.

  • Meetup group: the 2nd Sunday of each month, 12 to 2 PM, in person at a restaurant. Locations vary, so check the Meetup group page for each event.
  • St. Ann's: the 3rd Sunday of each month, right after the 11:15 AM Mass, at St. Ann Catholic Church, School Room 7, 17111 Route 1 (Jefferson Davis Highway), South Chesterfield, VA 23834.

Meetups are free, and donations are welcome. At the restaurant, you pay for your own food or drinks.

Register below and Cancer Coach USA will follow up with the next gathering. You can also join the group on Meetup to see events and RSVP there. Have questions? See the FAQ.
